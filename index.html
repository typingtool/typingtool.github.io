❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best free site for daily typing practice?

TypingTool Simulator is considered one of the best free sites for daily typing practice. It provides a real exam-like interface, tracks your WPM (Words Per Minute), accuracy, and highlights weak keys, making it perfect for daily consistent improvement.

How can I increase my typing speed to 40+ WPM?

To increase your typing speed, you must practice daily on a strict platform that disables copy-paste and backspace (if required by exam). Focus on accuracy first; speed will naturally follow. Our tool tracks your progress history so you can monitor your daily typing speed growth.

Is this useful for non-SSC students who just want to learn typing?

Absolutely! While designed with SSC CGL, CHSL, and Railway NTPC standards in mind, anyone who wants to learn fast touch typing or improve their general keyboard speed can use the "Custom Practice" mode. It supports both English and Hindi typing.

How does daily typing practice help?

Daily typing practice builds muscle memory. Practicing for just 15-20 minutes daily on our simulator will dramatically reduce your error rate and boost your KDPH (Key Depressions Per Hour) and WPM within a few weeks.
